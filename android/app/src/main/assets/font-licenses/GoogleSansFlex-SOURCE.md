# Google Sans Flex

`res/font/google_sans_flex.ttf` is derived from `GoogleSansFlex[ROND,opsz,slnt,wdth,wght].ttf`, version 5.000, at
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/googlesansflex, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(`GoogleSansFlex-OFL.txt`). Copyright 2015 The Google Sans Flex Authors.

Changes, made with fontTools: the width, roundness, slant and optical size axes are fixed at 100, 0, 0 and 18; weight
stays variable from 300 to 700; glyphs are limited to Basic Latin, Latin-1 Supplement, Latin Extended-A and -B, general
punctuation, currency signs and a few arrows and symbols; hinting is dropped.
